Ocarina of Time songs, played on a real ocarina

The five buttons in the game are five notes: D, F, A, B and high D. Learn these five fingerings on a 12-hole Alto C ocarina and you can play all 12 songs. In the 2026 Nintendo Switch 2 remake, the console microphone hears a real ocarina played this way and Link plays the song in the game.

The five fingerings

These match the fingering guide Nintendo published on the official remake site. A Soprano C ocarina uses the same fingerings and sounds an octave higher. STL Ocarina sells the Alto C size as "Tenor C". The drawing shows the top of the ocarina as you hold it; the two holes outside the body are the thumb holes on the back. Both small sub-holes stay open for all five notes.

  1. DN64 A · D5Switch 2 L

    Lift only your right little finger; cover the rest.

  2. FN64 C-Down · F5Switch 2 R

    Lift your right middle, ring and little fingers; cover the rest.

  3. AN64 C-Right · A5Switch 2 A

    Lift your left ring finger and all four right fingers; cover the rest.

  4. BN64 C-Left · B5Switch 2 Y

    Cover only your left index and little fingers, left thumb and right thumb; lift the rest.

  5. High DN64 C-Up · D6Switch 2 X

    Cover only your left little finger and right thumb; lift the rest.

Filled hole: cover itWhite hole: lift

Six warp songs

Sheik teaches these to adult Link. Each one warps you to the front of a temple.

  1. Minuet of ForestTo the Forest TempleD, High D, B, A, B, A
  2. Bolero of FireTo the Fire TempleF, D, F, D, A, F, A, F
  3. Serenade of WaterTo Lake HyliaD, F, A, A, B
  4. Requiem of SpiritTo the Desert ColossusD, F, D, A, F, D
  5. Nocturne of ShadowAbove Kakariko GraveyardB, A, A, D, B, A, F
  6. Prelude of LightTo the Temple of TimeHigh D, A, High D, A, B, High D

The Scarecrow's Song has no fixed notes. As young Link, play any eight of the five notes to the scarecrow at Lake Hylia, then play the same eight again as an adult.
