In the game

N64 buttons
A, C-Down, C-Up, A, C-Down, C-Up
Switch 2 buttons
L, R, X, L, R, X
Where to learn it
As an adult, you learn it from the man in the Kakariko Village windmill.
What it does
Brings rain and reveals hidden holes. Played as a child inside the windmill, it drains the well so you can climb down to the Bottom of the Well.

FAQ

What are the ocarina notes for Song of Storms?

Song of Storms is D, F, High D, D, F, High D. On a 12-hole Alto C ocarina that is D5 F5 D6 D5 F5 D6, using 3 different fingerings. Based on the button order of the 1998 Nintendo 64 game.

How fast is Song of Storms played?

The in-game performance lasts 3.0 seconds. The shortest note is 0.18 seconds and the last note is held for 1.5 seconds.

Can I play Song of Storms on a real ocarina in the Switch 2 remake?

Yes. The 2026 Nintendo Switch 2 version listens through the console microphone, so if you play Song of Storms with the fingerings on this page, Link plays the same song. It only recognizes songs you have already learned in the game.
